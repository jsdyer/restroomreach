What the map includes


These are OpenStreetMap objects tagged amenity=toilets inside configured regional rectangles. Cafés and other venues with toilets may be absent. Duplicate objects and incomplete or outdated tags can occur. A blank field means not recorded, not “no”.


Dates mean different things


The capture date is when this copy was downloaded. The OSM database date describes the source edition; an object’s last edit can be unrelated to its toilet details. Survey dates are shown only when a contributor recorded one.


Your data


Search, filters, typed coordinates and imported receipts stay in this tab. Nothing is saved to browser storage. “Use my location” asks your browser for permission and uses the result locally. Turning on the street map contacts OpenStreetMap’s tile server, which sees your IP address and the viewed area. Source links open their named services. Save a receipt before closing to keep a copy.


Regional records refresh weekly, with eight captures retained by default. GitHub schedules can be delayed or missed. Add regions and tune limits in web/config.json.
